A Nutrition International (NI) representative introduces the organisation's school-based adolescent nutrition programme, delivered in partnership with the Government of Bangladesh across ten districts. Highlights include a WASH-and-nutrition diary and a healthy-recipe booklet distributed to adolescent girls in roughly eighty schools, a video testimonial from a student on the benefits of iron-folic acid supplementation, and an overview of NI's global footprint (five countries in Asia, five in Africa). The speaker outlines NI's four core programme areas -- Vitamin A supplementation, salt iodisation support, work with the Institute of Public Health Nutrition, and a rice-fortification ("food-friendly") programme for poor and marginalised groups -- and stresses that NI's approach is to strengthen existing government programmes rather than run separate parallel ones.
