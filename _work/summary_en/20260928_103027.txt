A detailed technical presentation and discussion (~58 minutes) on adolescent anaemia and the school-based nutrition programme. Key data cited: 28.9% anaemia prevalence among adolescents nationally (National Micronutrient Survey), with a sub-group figure as high as 53.1%; iron-folic-acid tablet coverage cited at roughly 71% in an earlier period, falling to about 61% by 2020 due to supply interruptions. The speaker frames adolescence as a "second window of opportunity" for nutrition intervention and notes Bangladesh currently lacks a dedicated adolescent nutrition strategy, relying instead on two operational guidelines (in-school and out-of-school). Implementation details covered: fortified-biscuit distribution since 2022, teacher training, adolescent ("kishor-kishori") clubs, district-level coordination meetings, and reporting through a tracking system, with coordination alongside UNICEF in shared districts. The Q&A raised salt iodisation's link to iron-folic-acid supplementation, a cited study finding around 90% Vitamin D deficiency among roughly 5,000 sampled participants, and the ongoing government discussion about mandating broader rice fortification. Closing remarks name programme scale-up and supply-chain/inter-ministerial coordination as the two main outstanding challenges.
