The longest session in the set (~84 minutes), covering continued programme collaboration and a detailed district-level presentation from Jamalpur. The Jamalpur presentation reports programme activity across nine upazilas, with roughly 213 (and, in a related count, around 113) schools covered, each expected to run a functioning "kishor-kishori" (adolescent) club led by a teacher, delivering weekly iron-folic-acid supplementation alongside nutrition education. Implementation challenges highlighted include inconsistent school-level reporting and delays compiling data through upazila and district education offices, addressed partly through a digital reporting system and district coordinators. The session also covers stakeholder (student/parent/teacher) feedback gathered via video testimonial, coordination with UNICEF to divide coverage in shared districts, and a technical Q&A revisiting salt iodisation's link to iron-folic-acid policy, a cited ~90% Vitamin D deficiency finding, and rice-fortification policy. Closes with thanks to district coordinators and the education directorate ("MAUSHI") for the reporting partnership.
